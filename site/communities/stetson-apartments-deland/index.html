About Stetson Apartments

Welcome to Your New Home in the Heart of DeLand! This charming apartment is part of an intimate 11-unit multifamily building, combining historic character with modern comfort. Located just blocks from downtown DeLand, Stetson University, and a variety of shops and restaurants, this location offers the best of small-town living with city-style convenience. Enjoy easy access to local dining, entertainment, and campus life—all just a short walk away. Exclusive Bonus Amenity: Included in your lease is enrollment in our Utility & Maintenance Reduction Program, which provides HVAC filters delivered to your door every 60 days—helping you breathe easier and maintain a clean, energy-efficient home. Professionally managed by Atrium Management, we're here to make your living experience seamless and stress-free.
