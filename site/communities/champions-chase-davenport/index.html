About Champions Chase

Experience the best of luxury living at Champions Chase, a professionally managed townhome community conveniently located near Champions Gate in Davenport, Florida. These spacious, pet-friendly homes offer the freedom and feel of a single-family residence without the burdens of homeownership. Enjoy a community-focused lifestyle with all the modern amenities you desire, perfectly situated for easy access to area attractions, shopping, and dining. These townhomes have it all! There are 5 different models to choose from with expansive three to four-bedroom floorplans tailored to fit your lifestyle needs. Each home comes with a number of features including one to two-car garages that come with EV charging. Future-forward innovations like smart home technology. Stylish interior finishes like hardwood-style flooring, Samsung stainless-steel appliances, chef-inspired Kitchen, Custom Cabinetry, the airy feel of vaulted ceilings, and quaint views of the community through expansive windows. Also included are HIGH-SPEED Internet and Selected Premium Channels via Spectrum. For our furry residents, we have dedicated dog parks where they can run and play to their heart's content.
