About Valencia Isle

Experience the best of townhome living at Valencia Isle. This Beautifully Constructed Townhome Community offers a convenient location that puts you just minutes away from shopping, dining, and entertainment options. With easy access to major highways such as the SR-417 and 408. This Community is conveniently located just North of Valencia College East Campus. These townhomes have it all! There are 5 different models to choose from with expansive three to four-bedroom floorplans tailored to fit your lifestyle needs. Each home comes with a number of features including one to two-car garages that come with EV charging. Future-forward innovations like smart home technology. Stylish interior finishes like hardwood-style flooring, Samsung stainless-steel appliances, chef-inspired Kitchen, Custom Cabinetry, the airy feel of vaulted ceilings, and quaint views of the community through expansive windows. Also included are HIGH-SPEED Internet and Selected Premium Channels via Spectrum. For our furry residents, we have dedicated dog parks where they can run and play to their heart's content.
