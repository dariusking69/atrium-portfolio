About This Floor Plan

Welcome home to Woodland Heights, where we offer beautiful designed 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartments featuring modern luxury vinyl plank (LVP) flooring, a spacious open-concept layout, and the convenience of a washer and dryer included in every unit.
