About Woodland Heights Apartments

Welcome home to Woodland Heights, where we offer beautiful designed 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartments featuring modern luxury vinyl plank (LVP) flooring, a spacious open-concept layout, and the convenience of a washer and dryer included in every unit. Whether you prefer a first, second, or third-floor unit, Woodland Heights offers a floor plan to fit your lifestyle. Enjoy bright, inviting living spaces that are perfect for relaxing, entertaining, or working from home. Ideally located just minutes from downtown, close to Lake Hollingsworth and you'll have easy access to shopping, dining, entertainment, and major roadways. Plus, the YMCA right next door offers endless opportunities and extracurricular activities!
