About Harwood

Looking for a steal of a deal in one of Orlando’s most unbeatable locations? This charming, petite apartment at 317 E. Hardwood Unit 5 is your golden ticket to urban living with a quirky twist! Perfect for those who crave character and convenience, this gem is ready to be your launchpad to the vibrant pulse of the city. Let’s talk location—because this is where the magic happens. Nestled just minutes from the electric energy of Downtown Orlando, you’re practically a stone’s throw from the iconic skyline. Imagine strolling to the bustling cafes, trendy boutiques, and world-class entertainment that define the downtown scene. Catch a show at the Dr. Phillips Center for the Performing Arts, sip craft cocktails at a hidden speakeasy, or grab a bite from the endless array of eateries—all without breaking a sweat (or your wallet on parking!). The proximity to Downtown Orlando puts you right in the mix of festivals, farmers’ markets, and that unmistakable city vibe that makes this area a hotspot for locals and visitors alike. The apartment itself? Think cozy, vintage charm that’s begging for your personal touch. It’s small, it’s quirky, it’s dated in all the right ways—perfect for minimalists, creatives, or anyone who’d rather spend their money exploring the spectacular surroundings than on fancy finishes. At this price, you’re not just renting a space—you’re securing a front-row seat to one of Florida’s most dynamic neighborhoods. Don’t sleep on this rare find— living this close to Downtown Orlando doesn’t come around often.
