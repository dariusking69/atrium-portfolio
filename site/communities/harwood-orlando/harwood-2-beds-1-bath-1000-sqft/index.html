About This Floor Plan

Nestled on a serene, tree-lined brick street in the highly sought-after Lake Eola Heights Historic District, this delightful 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom cottage offers 1,000 square feet of cozy, single-story living. Just steps from the iconic Lake Eola Park, this detached single-family home is a rare gem in one of Orlando’s most architecturally rich and vibrant neighborhoods. Step inside to discover a light-filled interior with classic hardwood floors and high ceilings that enhance the home’s inviting ambiance. The open-concept living and dining area provides a flexible space perfect for entertaining or relaxing, with large windows that frame picturesque views of the historic neighborhood. Both bedrooms are generously sized, offering plenty of natural light and closet space, while the well-appointed bathroom features timeless fixtures. Outside, the cottage’s private lot includes a charming front porch, perfect for enjoying the neighborhood’s historic charm, and a low-maintenance backyard that offers a peaceful retreat. With its prime location, you’re just a short stroll from Lake Eola’s swan boats, weekly farmers market, and the eclectic dining and shopping of Thornton Park and Downtown Orlando. The neighborhood’s walkability, coupled with easy access to I-4 and top-rated Orange County schools, makes this home an ideal blend of convenience and lifestyle. Embrace the opportunity to own a piece of Orlando’s history in Lake Eola Heights, where Colonial Revival, Craftsman, and Art Deco architecture create a unique and timeless backdrop. This cottage is perfect for those seeking a blend of historic character and modern urban living.
