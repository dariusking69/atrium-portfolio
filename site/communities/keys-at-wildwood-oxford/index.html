About Keys at Wildwood

Welcome to The Keys at Wildwood, a brand-new residential sanctuary where the laid-back charm of the Florida Keys meets the modern convenience of Central Florida. Located just 2.5 miles from the world-renowned shopping and dining of The Villages, our gated community offers an upscale, maintenance-free lifestyle in the heart of Oxford/Wildwood. Our expertly designed villas redefine the rental experience. Built with high-quality concrete construction for superior noise reduction and energy efficiency, each home provides a tranquil retreat from the outside world. Inside, you’ll find spacious, single-level floor plans that evoke the true feeling of homeownership without the hassle. Premium Home Features: Gourmet Kitchens: Featuring granite countertops, shaker-style cabinetry, and high-end stainless steel appliances. Smart Home Integration: Every villa is equipped with an Ambient Smart Home package, including a smart hub, keyless entry, doorbell camera, and thermostat controls. Luxury Finishes: Enjoy 10-foot high ceilings, wide-plank flooring, and oversized walk-in closets. Convenience Included: Every home features a full-sized washer and dryer, private driveways, and a private patio for enjoying the Florida sunshine. At The Keys at Wildwood, your backyard is a vacation destination. Whether you’re cooling off in our exquisite resort-style pool, hitting a personal best in the fully-equipped fitness center, or letting your furry friend run free in our dedicated on-site dog park, there is something here for everyone. Pet-Friendly Living: We welcome your four-legged family members! Security & Service: Enjoy peace of mind with gated community access and the ultimate convenience of valet trash service. Prime Location: Positioned perfectly on US-301, you are minutes away from The Villages Regional Hospital, Aldi, Costco, and the vibrant entertainment at Brownwood Paddock Square. Are you looking for a luxury rental near The Villages? Experience the difference of a community built with you in mind.
