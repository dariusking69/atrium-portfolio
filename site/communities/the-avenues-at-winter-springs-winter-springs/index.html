About The Avenues at Winter Springs

Our pet-friendly community offers spacious 1, 2, and 3-bedroom apartment homes with modern finishes and everyday conveniences. Enjoy amenities like a resort-style pool with sundeck, 24-hour fitness and wellness center, tennis court, playground, and leash-free pet park. Stay connected with 400 Mbps internet, and take advantage of on-site management, garages, and short-term lease options. Select homes feature upgraded white cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, hardwood-inspired flooring, private patios/balconies, and wood-burning fireplaces.
