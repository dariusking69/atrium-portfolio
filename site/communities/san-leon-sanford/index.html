About San Leon

Welcome home to the San Leon apartments; Downtown Sanford's newest luxury community. Enjoy close convenience to Sanford River Walk, Lake Monroe, and the array of charming local businesses downtown. Here, everything you love about downtown is in close reach! Our homes are equipped with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, tiled backsplashes, vinyl floors, and in unit washer and dryer. Second and third floor apartments have balconies with sliding glass doors and shades for privacy. Residents will be given access to our pet washing station, private parking lot, and rentable onsite storage. This quaint community has everything you want in a downtown apartment and more!
