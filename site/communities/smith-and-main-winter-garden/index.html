About Smith & Main

LIMITED SPECIAL: Lease by 8/15/2026 and get your first full month RENT FREE! Limited time ONLY Discover Smith & Main, a stunning addition to the sought-after Downtown Winter Garden district. Built in 2023, this exclusive community features striking Mediterranean-style architecture that blends historic charm with contemporary comfort. Durable, stylish LVP flooring runs throughout. Select units feature private balconies or terraces for your personal oasis.
