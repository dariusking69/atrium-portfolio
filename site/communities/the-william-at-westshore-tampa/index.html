About The William at Westshore

Enjoy efficient, restored living in this 561-square-foot studio at The William at Westshore, featuring a private entry and hardwood-style flooring throughout. Upgraded homes offer granite- or quartz-style countertops and stainless steel appliances, with select units including a balcony or screened-in patio for extra outdoor space. Just outside your door, residents enjoy a resort-style pool, 24/7 fitness center, fire pit lounge, and outdoor grill kitchen in a quiet corner of Port Tampa City — 20 minutes from downtown Tampa with HART bus access nearby. Pet-friendly and smoke-free.
