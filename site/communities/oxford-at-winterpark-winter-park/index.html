About Oxford at Winterpark

***Up to $10,000 in Free Rent on Select Luxury Homes!*** Welcome to The Oxford at Winter Park, an exclusive luxury community situated in one of Central Florida's most sought-after locations. Thoughtfully designed for sophisticated living, these spacious multi-level single family homes feature open-concept layouts, abundant natural light, and modern architectural finishes throughout. Gourmet kitchens serve as the heart of each residence, complete with high-end appliances, elegant quartz countertops, custom cabinetry, and spacious islands perfect for entertaining. Unwind in generous primary suites, which offer peaceful retreats with expansive walk-in closets and elegant ensuite bathrooms. Additional interior highlights include private outdoor patio and balcony spaces, attached garage parking, high-end flooring, and energy-efficient designs. Beyond your front door, The Oxford offers a serene, beautifully maintained neighborhood setting professionally managed by Atrium Management Company. Residents enjoy seamless convenience paired with a quiet, boutique community atmosphere. Nestled just minutes from historic downtown Winter Park, iconic Park Avenue shopping and dining, scenic parks, and Rollins College, exceptional lifestyle options surround you. Experience the ideal blend of modern elegance, suburban tranquility, and urban accessibility at The Oxford at Winter Park.
