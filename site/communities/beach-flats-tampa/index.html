About Beach Flats

Start your Tampa story in a smartly designed Hyde Park studio, ranging from 329 to 492 square feet at Beach Flats. Each home is finished with custom tile and stainless steel appliances, and residents share access to a gated courtyard, fire-pit lounge, outdoor grill kitchen, and covered bike storage. It's a smoke-free, pet-friendly community just minutes from Bayshore Boulevard, Hyde Park Village, and the Riverwalk — perfect for professionals who want an efficient home base near everything South Tampa has to offer.
