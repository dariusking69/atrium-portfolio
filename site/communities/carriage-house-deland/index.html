About Carriage House

Discover historic charm and modern convenience at Carriage House Apartments, 916 W. New York Avenue — a beautifully renovated 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom apartment in the heart of Downtown DeLand. This updated unit features a fresh, contemporary interior, on-site community laundry, and optional 1 Gigabit high-speed internet for $60/month. You're just steps from Main Street's historic charm, diverse dining, the DeLand Mural and Sculpture Walks, the Athens Theatre, and nearby Blue Spring and De Leon Springs State Parks. Please note: some photos are virtually staged to showcase the space's potential.
