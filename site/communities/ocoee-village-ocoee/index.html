About Ocoee Village

*EIGHTH WEEKS FREE ON ALL CARTER MODELS! 8th weeks free on all available models if applied and move-in by August 31! Already Prorated through the term of the lease.* Experience the best of townhome living at Ocoee Village. This Brand-New Community offers a convenient location that puts you just minutes away from shopping, dining, and entertainment options. With easy access to major highways such as the SR-429 full intersection. Providing a direct route to Disney World! These townhomes have it all! There are 5 different models to choose from with expansive three to four-bedroom floorplans tailored to fit your lifestyle needs. Each home comes with a number of features including one to two-car garages that come with EV charging. Future-forward innovations like smart home technology. Stylish interior finishes like hardwood-style flooring, Samsung stainless-steel appliances, chef-inspired Kitchen, Custom Cabinetry, the airy feel of vaulted ceilings, and quaint views of the community through expansive windows. Also included are HIGH-SPEED Internet and Selected Premium Channels via Spectrum. On weekends, hang out in our sun-drenched, resort-style pool with your favorite people or unwind with a good book in our cabana area. Our playground is the perfect spot for children to burn off some energy and make new friends. And for our furry residents, we have dedicated dog parks where they can run and play to their heart's content. (Pet Fees Applied) This community is professionally managed by Atrium Management.
