The Fern – Modern 1 Bedroom, 1.5 Bathroom | 784 sq.ft. Welcome to The Fern at The Avenue at Baldwin Park, a thoughtfully designed 1-bedroom, 1.5-bathroom apartment offering 784 sq.ft. of stylish comfort and functionality. This spacious layout features an open-concept living and dining area, perfect for entertaining or relaxing at home. The primary suite includes a private full bathroom and generous closet space, while the convenient half bath is ideal for guests.
