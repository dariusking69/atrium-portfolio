About Coliseum Lofts

Looking for the perfect place to live, study, and thrive? Coliseum Lofts is the ultimate housing choice for busy VCU undergrads. Just steps from campus, our loft-style apartments blend historic charm with modern convenience-think exposed brick, open layouts, and oversized closets that let you live large. Enjoy all-inclusive utilities (yes, even basic TV!), free internet, and unbeatable access to VCU's academic buildings, library, and athletic facilities-so you can skip the commute and focus on what matters. Coliseum Lofts isn't just where you live-it's where you level up your college experience.
