About This Property

Welcome home to this beautifully maintained 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom first-floor corner unit condo, offering the perfect combination of comfort, convenience, and resort-style living. The spacious kitchen is equipped with granite countertops, modern cabinetry, a convenient breakfast bar with stylish pendant lighting, a pantry for extra storage, and like-new appliances. Ceramic tile flooring flows throughout the home, providing both durability and easy maintenance. Both bathrooms also feature granite countertops, creating a cohesive and upscale feel. Enjoy the convenience of a large laundry room with a full-size washer and dryer included, along with energy-efficient lighting and ceiling fans throughout the home. Step outside to your private screened-in patio, the perfect place to relax with your morning coffee or unwind after a long day. One of the best features of this home is the value included in your rent. Water, sewer, trash are all included, helping simplify your monthly expenses. Residents enjoy access to outstanding community amenities, including a resort-style lap pool, Jacuzzi, fitness center, clubhouse, volleyball courts, BBQ and picnic area, playground, walking trail, and car wash center.
