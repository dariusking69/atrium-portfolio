About This Property

3 Bedroom 2 Bath home for rent in Country Club of Mount Dora, a Golf Course Community. This 3 Bedroom 2 Bath home offers a bright and airy feel throughout with great windows and natural light. The home is within the Country Club of Mount Dora, a vibrant and active community. You'll enjoy the pool and screened lanai, offering great room for entertaining. The home's split floor plan featuring a spacious living room and large eat in kitchen. The great room is just off the other side of the kitchen. The master bedroom features ensuite bathroom with dual closets. The front guest bedroom at the front of the home has a built-in murphy bed, great for overnight guests. If you're seeking southern charm and an active Florida lifestyle, the Country Club of Mount Dora is just the right place. The community is lined with sidewalks for walking, jogging and biking. Benches are placed throughout to sit and enjoy the scenery and to rest and plenty of playgrounds for the little ones. The Country Club of Mount Dora is located just minutes from Mount Dora's famous downtown. Often referred to as 'Festival City', Mount Dora's Downtown features great shopping, dining, entertainment and recreational activities. Situated on the shores of Lake Dora on the Harris Chain of Lakes, you'll enjoy breath taking sunsets over the water. Pets are considered with owner approval; additional fees may apply.
