About This Property

Welcome to your Tranquil 3 Bedroom 2.5 Bath Rural Retreat with Den and upstairs loft space, located in the serene locale of Polk County. This charming house offers a peaceful escape from the hustle and bustle of city life. With three spacious bedrooms and two and a half bathrooms, this home provides ample space for you to relax and unwind. The open floor plan creates a seamless flow between the living room, dining area, and kitchen, making it perfect for entertaining guests or spending quality time with loved ones. The well-appointed kitchen features modern appliances and plenty of cabinet space, making meal preparation a breeze. Outside, you'll find large outdoor space perfect for enjoying the Florida sunshine or hosting outdoor gatherings. This home is conveniently located near Poinciana Community Park, Allen David Broussard Catfish Creek Preserve, Legoland, and Bok Tower, offering endless opportunities for outdoor recreation and entertainment.
