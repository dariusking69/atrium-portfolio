About This Property

Welcome home to this spacious and beautifully designed townhome in the highly sought-after Ravenna community of Winter Garden! This exceptional floor plan offers 4 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and a two-car garage, featuring two private primary suites—one conveniently located on the first floor and another upstairs—providing the perfect layout for multi-generational living, guests, or added privacy. The open-concept main living area is highlighted by elegant tile flooring throughout the living room, dining area, and kitchen. The gourmet kitchen is designed to impress with stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, abundant cabinetry, and a large center island with breakfast bar seating, making it ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs, you'll find a spacious loft that can be used as a second living area, home office, or playroom, along with three additional bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and a conveniently located laundry closet complete with a washer and dryer. Ideally located with easy access to Downtown Winter Garden, the Hamlin shopping district, Walt Disney World, and major roadways, this home offers the perfect combination of comfort, style, and convenience.
