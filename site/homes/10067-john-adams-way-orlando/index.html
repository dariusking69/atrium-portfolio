About This Property

Welcome to a home designed for grand living and ultimate comfort in a prime Orlando location! This extraordinary 6-bedroom, 5-bathroom residence offers an unparalleled lifestyle with an abundance of space and luxurious amenities. Step inside and discover two distinct living areas, one on each floor, providing ample room for relaxation and entertaining. This home truly understands the meaning of convenience, featuring two master bedrooms—one on each level—and two dedicated laundry rooms. Entertainment is redefined here with your very own cinema room, perfect for movie nights or big game days. The upstairs boasts a stylish bar top, ideal for hosting gatherings. The heart of this home is its large, gourmet kitchen, offering vast counter space and storage for culinary enthusiasts. Enjoy spacious rooms throughout, ensuring comfort for everyone. Outside, the expansive backyard is a private oasis, complemented by two separate driveways for plentiful parking. Ideally located in the Presidents Pointe subdivision, this home offers easy access to a vibrant array of amenities. You're just minutes from Waterford Lakes Town Center, boasting a fantastic selection of shops like Target, Publix, and various specialty stores.
