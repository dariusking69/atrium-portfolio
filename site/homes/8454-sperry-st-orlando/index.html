About This Property

Welcome to this beautifully appointed luxury corner-unit townhome in the highly sought-after Laureate Park community of Lake Nona. Offering over 2,000 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, this Ashton Woods model features a unique three-story layout with 3 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and upscale finishes throughout. High-speed internet is included, adding even more convenience to this exceptional home. The first floor offers a private bedroom with its own en suite bathroom, making it the perfect space for guests, a home office, or multigenerational living. Upstairs, the spacious second floor serves as the heart of the home, featuring an open-concept living and dining area, a beautifully appointed kitchen with granite countertops, a large center island, and ample cabinet space. A convenient half bath is also located on this level, along with access to a private balcony—ideal for enjoying your morning coffee or relaxing in the evening. The third floor is home to two additional spacious bedroom suites, each featuring its own private en suite bathroom for maximum comfort and privacy. A centrally located laundry room completes the upper level for added convenience. As a desirable corner unit, this home enjoys additional windows that fill the interior with natural light while providing an open and airy feel. Ideally located just one block from the Laureate Park Aquatic Center, LP Fit, Canvas Restaurant, and community parks, residents enjoy an unmatched lifestyle with access to resort-style amenities. Experience luxury, convenience, and one of Central Florida's most desirable communities in this exceptional Laureate Park townhome, where internet is included and resort-style living awaits.
