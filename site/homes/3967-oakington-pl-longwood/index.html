About This Property

Beautiful 2 story, 3 bedroom w/bonus loft and 2.5 bath townhome sitting on the 17th tee of the golf course in Wekiva Landings. This home faces west and overlooks the golf course and pond with fabulous sunsets. Featuring an open floor plan, enjoy an exquisite kitchen with stainless steel appliances, granite counters and breakfast bar, 42" Maple cabinets with crown molding, and dining area. Additionally, you'll find beautiful high ceilings, quality laminate flooring in all living areas, and tile in the bathrooms/laundry room. The steps are carpeted. There is a large master bedroom on the first floor including ensuite bathroom with granite, double sinks and large step in shower. As well as two additional bedrooms, bonus loft overlooking the first floor, and an additional full bathroom on the second floor. Experience the ultimate in comfortable and convenient living in this desirable Wekiva Landings Community. Step outside into the screened Lanai overlooking a beautifully landscaped backyard and the 17th tee, where you can unwind and enjoy the Florida sunshine and golf course. This home is just moments away from an array of shopping, dining, and entertainment options. With its prime location and impeccable design, this Longwood home offers the perfect blend of style and functionality.
