About This Property

Welcome to this charming 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom house located in the peaceful town of Oxford, FL on multiple acres shared with a horse farm. This lovely home offers a comfortable and inviting living space for anyone looking to settle down in a serene environment. With three spacious bedrooms, there is plenty of room for everyone to enjoy their own personal space. The two bathrooms provide convenience and privacy for all residents. The house is situated in a desirable location, offering a peaceful retreat away from the hustle and bustle of city life. Whether you own horses or other livestock or just enjoy animals, this property is perfect for those seeking a cozy and manageable living space. A portion of the property is used to operate a horse farm and the other side is this residential property with access to chicken coops, a fully enclosed paddock, and outdoor stables. This home also features an attached two car garage, long driveway, as well as a screen enclosed porch overlooking horse pastures and fields. The house is waiting for you to make it your own.
