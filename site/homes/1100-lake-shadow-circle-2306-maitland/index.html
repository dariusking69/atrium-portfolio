About This Property

Charming 2-Bedroom, 2-Bathroom Condo with Resort-Style Amenities in Gated Maitland Community Welcome to 1100 Lake Shadow Circle, Unit #2306 – a beautifully maintained condo that offers a perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience. This spacious unit features two generous bedroom suites, each with large walk-in closets and private bathrooms. Step outside to your private balcony, complete with additional outdoor storage. The bright and airy living room overlooks the dining area and kitchen. The well-appointed kitchen leads to a utility closet with extra storage and an interior laundry room, including a full-sized washer and dryer for your convenience. High ceilings enhance the peaceful atmosphere, with only one neighboring unit and no noisy footsteps above you. Ideal for both relaxation and recreation, this condo is packed with resort-style amenities. Enjoy sunny days by one of two sparkling community pools with scenic lake views, or stay active with access to the basketball and tennis courts. Fitness enthusiasts will appreciate the 24-hour gym, which includes a kids’ center to keep little ones entertained while you work out. Additional amenities include scenic walking trails, a clubhouse with a catering kitchen, a business center, and a car wash area. The secure, gated environment provides peace of mind, while the nearby schools offer excellent educational opportunities. Additionally, with Rolling College, Seminole State College and Valencia College nearby, it offers further educational opportunities for students of all ages. Whether you're seeking a peaceful retreat or a place to stay connected to the pulse of the city, you'll find it here, along with nearby attractions like Winter Park for shopping, dining, and entertainment.
